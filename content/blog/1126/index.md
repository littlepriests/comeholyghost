november was an active month, relatively speaking. my solar return looms and will mark my second birthday out in cornfield, north america. despite whatever preconceptions ppl have about the rural midwest, i've had my fair share of exposure to the new, the unexpected, and the beautiful. where is the beauty! apparently it's somewhere near [the cross at the crossroads](https://crossusa.org/).

## fabric and concrete

my sister picked up some artwork in stl for an upcoming exhibition at her museum, so i tagged along as entertainment. (the drive is two hours, maybe 2.5 hours.) 

she wanted to check out the pulitzer arts foundation, which was hosting an exhibition for [olga de amaral](https://olgadeamaral.art/), a colombian fiber artist. 

i went into the exhibition blind, both ignorant of amaral and having very little knowledge about textiles, let alone experimental/abstract fabric art. an immediate impression is that amaral's work is bizarre, a thought that returned any time i tried to make sense of what was in front of me.
